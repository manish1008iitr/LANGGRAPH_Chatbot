## **Document Overview** 

This handbook is organized into independent policy sections. Each section is written with explicit rules, conditions, timelines, and exceptions to make the content suitable for semantic retrieval and policy-based question answering. 

#### **Core Service Principles** 

- Provide customers with clear and understandable information before and after purchase. 

- Apply published policies consistently while reviewing exceptional circumstances fairly. 

- Protect customer information and use it only for legitimate business purposes. 

- Communicate order, delivery, cancellation, return, and refund status promptly. 

- Avoid making promises that are not supported by the applicable policy. 

- Escalate disputes, suspected fraud, and unusual cases to the appropriate support team. 

#### **Policy Interpretation Rules** 

1. The most recent approved version of a policy takes precedence over older versions. 

2. Product-specific terms may apply when they are displayed on the product page or invoice. 

3. If two policy provisions appear to conflict, the customer support team must escalate the case before making a final commitment. 

4. A support agent must verify order details before sharing account-specific information. 

5. Policy exceptions require documented approval from an authorized supervisor.

## **1. Customer Account Policy** 

#### **1.1 Account Creation** 

Customers may create a ShopEase account using a valid email address or mobile number. Customers must provide accurate information and are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their login credentials. 

- One individual should not create multiple accounts to misuse promotional benefits. 

- Customers must not use another person’s identity or payment credentials without authorization. 

- ShopEase may request additional verification when unusual account activity is detected. 

- A customer may browse products without an account, but certain services require sign-in. 

#### **1.2 Account Security** 

Customers should use a strong, unique password and avoid sharing one-time passwords, passwords, or authentication links. ShopEase support representatives will not request a customer’s full password. 

- Report suspected unauthorized access as soon as possible. 

- Change the password immediately if account compromise is suspected. 

- Do not share OTPs with callers, messages, or third parties claiming to represent ShopEase. 

- Customers are responsible for reviewing account activity and saved addresses. 

#### **1.3 Account Suspension and Closure** 

ShopEase may temporarily restrict or close an account where there is credible evidence of fraud, abusive behavior, repeated policy manipulation, unlawful activity, or threats to platform security. Customers may request a review through customer support. 

#### **1.4 Customer Responsibilities** 

- Keep contact information updated. 

- Use accurate delivery and billing details. 

- Review order summaries before completing payment. 

- Cooperate with reasonable verification requests. 

- Avoid abusive, discriminatory, threatening, or harassing communication. 

## **2. Product Browsing and Ordering Policy** 

#### **2.1 Product Information** 

ShopEase attempts to display accurate product titles, descriptions, images, specifications, prices, and availability. Minor differences in color, packaging, measurements, or appearance may occur because of screen settings, manufacturing variations, or updated packaging. 

#### **2.2 Product Availability** 

- Products shown as available may become unavailable before an order is completed. 

- Adding an item to a cart does not reserve inventory. 

- An order is confirmed only after the platform generates an order confirmation. 

- ShopEase may cancel an order when inventory information is materially incorrect.

#### **2.5 Quantity Restrictions** 

ShopEase may limit quantities for high-demand products, promotional products, or products subject to supply constraints. Orders that appear to bypass such restrictions may be reviewed or cancelled. 

## **3. Payment Policy** 

#### **3.1 Accepted Payment Methods** 

Depending on location and product eligibility, ShopEase may support credit cards, debit cards, net banking, UPI, selected digital wallets, cash on delivery, and other payment methods displayed at checkout. 

#### **3.2 Payment Authorization** 

- A payment authorization does not always mean that an order has been dispatched. 

- The payment provider may temporarily place a hold on funds. 

- Failed, reversed, or pending payments may require confirmation from the payment provider. 

- Customers should not attempt repeated payments if the first transaction is still processing. 

#### **3.3 Cash on Delivery** 

Cash on delivery may be available only for eligible products, addresses, and order values. Customers should keep the required amount ready and inspect the package for visible external damage before accepting it where inspection is permitted. 

#### **3.4 Failed or Duplicate Payments** 

If a customer is charged more than once for one order, the customer should contact support with transaction references. ShopEase will investigate payment records and coordinate with the payment provider. Duplicate authorizations that were not captured may be released automatically according to the provider’s timeline. 

#### **3.5 Payment Security** 

- ShopEase does not ask customers to share full card numbers, PINs, passwords, or OTPs through chat. 

- Payment details are processed through authorized payment partners where applicable. 

- Customers should complete payments only through official ShopEase checkout channels. 

- Requests to transfer money to an individual employee account should be treated as suspicious. 

## **4. Shipping and Delivery Policy** 

#### **4.1 Delivery Estimates** 

Delivery estimates are calculated using inventory location, destination, carrier capacity, serviceability, weather, public holidays, and other operational factors. An estimate is not an unconditional guarantee. 

|Delivery category|Typicalestimate|
|---|---|
|Metro standard delivery|2–5 business days|

|Remote orspecialservice areas|7–12business days|



#### **4.2 Dispatch and Tracking** 

- Customers receive a dispatch notification when the order is handed to the carrier. 

- Tracking information may take up to 24 hours to show movement. 

- A tracking status of ‘label created’ does not necessarily mean the parcel has begun transit. 

- Customers should use the tracking link supplied in official ShopEase communications. 

#### **4.3 Delivery Attempts** 

Carriers may make up to three delivery attempts, depending on local operating procedures. Customers should ensure that the address, phone number, and recipient availability are correct. A parcel may be returned to the seller after unsuccessful attempts or prolonged non-collection. 

#### **4.4 Delayed Delivery** 

When delivery exceeds the estimated window, customers may contact support for a tracking review. ShopEase may coordinate with the carrier, provide an updated estimate, or offer an eligible resolution when a shipment is confirmed lost. 

#### **4.5 Address Changes** 

Address changes can generally be requested only before dispatch. After dispatch, customers may contact support, but changes are subject to carrier capability and may not be possible.

#### **5.1 Customer-Initiated Cancellation** 

Customers may request cancellation through the order page or customer support before the order is dispatched. Cancellation is not guaranteed after dispatch, handover to a carrier, or preparation for final delivery.

#### **5.3 Cancellation After Dispatch**

#### **5.4 ShopEase-Initiated Cancellation**

#### **5.5 Refund Following Cancellation**

## **6. Returns and Replacement Policy** 

#### **6.1 General Return Window** 

Eligible products may be returned within 7 calendar days of delivery. The return window begins on the date the carrier records delivery. Some categories may have shorter windows, longer windows, or no return eligibility, as displayed on the product page. 

#### **6.2 Basic Eligibility Conditions** 

- The return request must be submitted within the stated return window. 

- The product should be unused unless the claim concerns a defect or damage. 

- Original packaging, accessories, manuals, tags, and invoices should be retained where applicable. 

- The product must not show customer-caused damage, misuse, or unauthorized modification. 

- The reason selected in the return request must accurately describe the issue. 

#### **6.3 Replacement Instead of Refund** 

For eligible products, customers may be offered a replacement when the item is defective, damaged, incomplete, or materially different from the listing. Replacement depends on inventory availability and verification of the reported issue. 

#### **6.4 Non-Returnable Categories** 

- Personalized or customized products, unless defective or incorrectly produced. 

- Products that cannot be resold for hygiene or safety reasons, where legally and operationally applicable. 

- Digital products or downloadable services after access has been used, unless otherwise specified. 

- Gift cards or vouchers, except where required by applicable law or platform policy. 

- Products damaged through misuse, improper storage, or unauthorized repair. 

#### **6.5 Return Inspection** 

Returned items may be inspected to verify the reported reason, product identity, accessories, serial number, and condition. Inspection does not automatically guarantee approval; the final decision depends on the applicable policy and evidence.

## **8. Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Products** 

#### **8.1 Reporting Timeframe** 

Customers should report visible transit damage, missing components, incorrect products, or major defects as soon as possible and preferably within 48 hours of delivery. Reports after this period may still be reviewed, but additional evidence may be required. 

#### **8.2 Evidence Requirements** 

- Clear photographs of the outer packaging and product. 

- A photograph of the shipping label where available. 

- A description of the issue and when it was discovered. 

- A short video when the issue involves operation or functionality. 

- Information about missing accessories, parts, or documentation. 

#### **8.3 Incorrect Product** 

If a customer receives a different product, size, model, color, or quantity from the confirmed order, ShopEase will review the order record and arrange an eligible replacement, return, or refund. 

#### **8.4 Defective Product** 

A product may be classified as defective when it fails to perform its advertised basic function under normal intended use. Troubleshooting may be requested for technical products, provided that troubleshooting does not create a safety risk. 

#### **8.5 Safety-Related Issues** 

Customers should stop using a product if it presents a possible safety hazard, overheating, electrical fault, leakage, or other dangerous condition. Customers should keep the product away from children and contact ShopEase support for instructions. Customers should not attempt unauthorized repairs.

## **10. Discounts, Coupons, and Promotional Offers** 

#### **10.1 Coupon Usage** 

- Coupons must be used before their stated expiry date. 

- A coupon may require a minimum cart value or specific product eligibility. 

- Some coupons may be limited to one use per customer or account. 

- Coupons cannot be exchanged for cash unless expressly stated. 

- Coupons may not be combined unless the offer specifically permits stacking. 

#### **10.2 Promotional Pricing** 

Promotional prices may be available for a limited period or while inventory lasts. ShopEase may correct obvious technical or pricing errors. A promotion may exclude delivery fees, taxes, or selected products, as stated in the offer terms. 

#### **10.3 Misuse of Offers** 

ShopEase may cancel or reverse promotional benefits obtained through multiple accounts, automated abuse, manipulated transactions, false information, or other prohibited activity. 

#### **10.4 Refunds Involving Discounts** 

When an order using a coupon is partially cancelled or returned, the refund may reflect the allocation of the discount across eligible items. The amount refunded may not equal the original item price if a conditional discount is no longer applicable. 

#### **10.5 Referral Programs** 

Referral rewards are issued only when the qualifying conditions are met. Self-referrals, duplicate accounts, fraudulent activity, and transactions that are cancelled or refunded may not qualify. 

## **11. Privacy and Data Protection Policy** 

#### **11.1 Information Collected** 

ShopEase may collect information required to provide services, such as contact details, delivery addresses, order history, payment status, support communications, device information, and usage data. The categories collected depend on the service used. 

#### **11.2 Use of Information**

- Respond to customer support requests. 

- Prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized account access. 

- Improve website performance, product discovery, and customer experience. 

- Send service-related notifications and, where permitted, marketing communications. 

#### **11.3 Data Minimization** 

Employees and support agents should access only the information necessary for their assigned task. Sensitive information must not be copied into unsecured documents, personal messaging applications, or unauthorized systems. 

#### **11.4 Customer Rights and Requests** 

Subject to applicable law and operational requirements, customers may request access, correction, deletion, or clarification regarding their personal information. Requests may require identity verification to protect the account holder. 

#### **11.5 Data Retention** 

ShopEase retains information only for as long as reasonably required for service delivery, legal compliance, dispute resolution, fraud prevention, accounting, and legitimate operational purposes. 

#### **11.6 Security Incidents** 

If ShopEase identifies a suspected data security incident, it will follow its internal incident response procedures, assess the impact, restrict unauthorized access, and provide legally required notifications.

## **13. Fraud Prevention and Acceptable Use** 

#### **13.1 Prohibited Activities** 

- Using stolen payment methods or another person’s account without authorization. 

- Creating multiple accounts to obtain unauthorized promotional benefits. 

- Submitting false claims about non-delivery, damage, or missing items. 

- Manipulating reviews, ratings, or seller communications. 

- Using bots or automated tools to disrupt services or bypass purchase limits. 

- Attempting to access systems, accounts, or data without authorization. 

#### **13.2 Fraud Review** 

ShopEase may place an order on hold while reviewing unusual payment patterns, inconsistent identity information, suspicious delivery addresses, repeated claims, or other risk indicators. A review may delay processing. 

#### **13.3 Customer Cooperation** 

Customers may be asked to confirm selected information or provide documentation. ShopEase will not request a customer’s password, full payment PIN, or one-time password. 

#### **13.4 Consequences** 

Where evidence supports a violation, ShopEase may cancel orders, reverse unauthorized benefits, restrict account functionality, suspend accounts, or refer matters to relevant authorities where appropriate. 

#### **13.5 False Claims** 

A customer who knowingly submits false or misleading information may lose eligibility for certain services and may be responsible for resulting costs, subject to applicable law. 

## **14. Frequently Asked Questions** 

#### **Can I cancel my order after placing it?** 

You can request cancellation before dispatch. After dispatch, cancellation may not be available and you may need to refuse delivery or submit an eligible return request. 

#### **How long do refunds take?** 

Refund initiation usually occurs within two business days after approval. The time for funds to appear depends on the payment provider and is commonly five to seven business days for cards or bank accounts. 

#### **Can I return every product?** 

No. Return eligibility depends on product category, condition, time window, and the terms shown on the product page. 

#### **What should I do if my package is damaged?** 

Take photographs of the packaging and product, avoid using the item if it may be unsafe, and report the issue as soon as possible, preferably within 48 hours. 

#### **Can I change my delivery address?** 

Address changes are generally possible only before dispatch and are subject to serviceability and operational constraints. 

#### **Why is my tracking information not moving?** 

Tracking updates may take up to 24 hours after dispatch. If the shipment remains unchanged beyond the estimated window, contact support for a review. 

#### **What if I receive the wrong product?** 

Contact support with the order details and photographs. ShopEase will review the mismatch and arrange an eligible replacement, return, or refund. 

#### **Can I use more than one coupon?** 

Only when the promotional terms allow coupon stacking. Otherwise, the system may accept only one applicable coupon. 

#### **Does ShopEase ask for OTPs?** 

No. Customers should never share OTPs, passwords, PINs, or full payment credentials with support representatives or other callers. 

#### **How do I escalate a complaint?** 

Provide your existing support reference number and request escalation when the issue remains unresolved or requires supervisor review. 

#### **What happens if a product is out of stock after payment?** 

ShopEase may cancel the order and initiate a refund according to the payment method and refund timeline. 

**Can I return a used product?** 

Used products are generally not eligible for standard returns, but defective, damaged, or incorrect products may be reviewed under the applicable exception process.
